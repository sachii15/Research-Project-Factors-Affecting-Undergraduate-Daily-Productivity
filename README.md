# Factors Affecting Daily Productivity among Undergraduate Students

## Overview

This research project investigates the factors that affect the daily
productivity of undergraduate students in private higher education
institutes in the Colombo District.

## Research Objectives

- Identify the key factors affecting undergraduate students' productivity.
- Analyze the relationship between different factors and daily productivity.
- Understand the challenges that influence students' academic productivity.
- Provide insights based on the collected data.

## Research Methodology

- **Research Type:** Quantitative Research
- **Target Population:** Undergraduate students in private higher education institutes
- **Location:** Colombo District
- **Target Sample Size:** 250 respondents
- **Data Collection:** Questionnaire-based survey
- **Data Analysis:** Statistical analysis

## Key Areas Investigated

- Time management
- Academic workload
- Sleep and rest
- Technology usage
- Study environment
- Personal factors
- Academic performance
- Daily productivity
